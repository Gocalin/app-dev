# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: Transformers

![Transformers Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/6/66/Transformers07.jpg)

## 🧠 Overview
**Transformers** is a 2007 science fiction **action film** directed by *Michael Bay* and produced by *Steven Spielberg*.  
It is based on the **Hasbro** toy line and features giant alien robots that can transform into vehicles.

---

## 🌍 Plot Summary
> "Before time began, there was the Cube..."  
> — Optimus Prime

The story follows **Sam Witwicky**, a teenager who discovers that his car is actually an alien robot from a distant planet.  
Two factions of alien robots — the **Autobots** and the **Decepticons** — battle on Earth for control of the *AllSpark*, a powerful artifact that can create new life.

---

## 🤖 Main Characters
- **Optimus Prime** – Leader of the Autobots  
- **Bumblebee** – Sam’s guardian and loyal Autobot  
- **Megatron** – Leader of the Decepticons  
- **Sam Witwicky** – Human protagonist played by *Shia LaBeouf*  
- **Mikaela Banes** – Sam’s friend and love interest, played by *Megan Fox*
